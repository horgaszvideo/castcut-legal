# CastCut Studio – Data Processing Terms (mode "A")

**Version:** 1.0 · Effective: 14 October 2026 · Annex to the GTC; concluded together with acceptance of the GTC (GDPR
art. 28). Translation; the Hungarian text ([HU](../hu/adatfeldolgozas.md)) prevails.

## 1. Parties and subject
- **Controller:** the Business (GTC 1.2).
- **Processor:** Laszlo Virag, horgaszvideo.bot@gmail.com (operator of CastCut Studio).
- **Subject:** running the Business's project on the Provider's computer, making videos and post texts.

## 2. Details of the processing
- **Data subjects:** the bot's users (owner, staff, helpers); people in the footage (guests, customers, staff).
- **Data:** Telegram id, name, role; messages, voice messages and their transcripts; photos, videos (image, voice);
  data in the videos and texts.
- **Purpose:** making and delivering the Business's promotional videos – nothing else (except the presentation use
  under GTC 8.3, which the Provider carries out on his own responsibility).
- **Duration:** while the GTC contract lasts, then until the export is handed over.

## 3. Obligations of the Processor
3.1. Processes the data only on the Business's instructions (given in the bot or by e-mail).

3.2. Only he and the users chosen by the Business have access to the data; he is bound by confidentiality.

3.3. **Security measures:** the project runs in a separate environment; keys are kept in the macOS keychain; the
control panel is reachable only locally, with a token; the bot reaches Drive only in the project's folder and only
by copying; working copies are deleted at the end of a job; the computer's disk is encrypted (FileVault).

3.4. **Sub-processors** (approved by the Business by accepting the GTC): Anthropic (Claude – during editing), Google
(Drive – storage), Telegram (messaging). The Provider gives notice before adding a new sub-processor; the Business
may object and then terminate the contract.

3.5. Helps the Business fulfil data subjects' requests (access, deletion, etc.).

3.6. In case of a **personal data breach**, notifies the Business without undue delay, at the latest within 48 hours
of becoming aware of it, so that the Business can keep the 72-hour notification deadline.

3.7. When the contract ends, hands over the project export at the Business's request, then deletes the data from his
computer (except where retention is mandatory); the Business's Drive folder is not affected.

3.8. On request, provides the information needed to demonstrate compliance with these obligations.

## 4. Obligations of the Business
The Business is responsible for the lawfulness of the processing, in particular for the consent of the people in
the footage and for informing its users (CastCut Studio's privacy notice may be used for this).
