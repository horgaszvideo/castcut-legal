# CastCut Studio – Privacy Notice for bot users

**Version:** 1.1 · **Effective:** 14 October 2026 · Translation; the Hungarian text
([HU](../hu/adatkezeles.md)) prevails.

This notice is for anyone who writes to the Telegram bot of a CastCut Studio project (owner, staff, helper), or whose
image or voice is in footage sent to it.

## 1. Who processes the data?

- **Controller:** the business whose bot it is (named in the bot's description). It decides which
  videos are made and what is published.
- **Processor** (in mode "A", when the project is run by the maker of CastCut Studio): Laszlo Virag, contact:
  horgaszvideo.bot@gmail.com. Processes the data on behalf of and on the instructions of the business.
- **Own purposes:** the maker of CastCut Studio acts as an independent controller for (a) the record of acceptance
  of the terms and (b) using finished videos and excerpts to present CastCut Studio (under section 8.3 of the GTC
  with the business; private persons are not named).
- In mode "B" (the business runs it on its own computer) only the business processes the data.

## 2. Which data, for what purpose?

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Telegram id, name, username, role (owner/staff) | checking who may write to the bot and who may approve |
| Messages, voice messages and their transcripts | planning and making the videos |
| Photos, videos, and the people seen/heard in them | making the videos; presentation use (section 1) |
| Approvals, acceptance of the terms (time, version) | proof of decisions and acceptance |

**Legal basis:** performance of the contract between the business and CastCut Studio, and the business's
legitimate interest (GDPR art. 6(1)(b) and (f)); for people in the footage, the consent obtained by the business.

## 3. Where is the data, who can see it?

- On the project's computer (in mode "A" the CastCut Studio maker's), separated from other projects.
- In the business's own Google Drive folder (footage, finished packages, backup).
- While editing, the **Anthropic (Claude)** service used by the project sees the processed parts (frames,
  transcripts, messages). Speech recognition runs locally on the computer.
- **Telegram** carries the messages; **Google** stores the Drive content.
- If the business switches on posting through Buffer, the finished, approved video and the post text go to the
  business's own **Buffer** account; for this the video file in the business's Drive is readable by anyone with the
  link until it is posted, then private again.
- These providers may process data outside the European Union, under their own terms and transfer safeguards.
- The business publishes the finished videos; from there the platforms' rules apply.

## 4. For how long?

- Working copies (previews, frames): deleted when a job is closed.
- Footage, finished videos, conversation log: until the business asks for deletion or the cooperation ends; then the
  business receives an export and the project is removed from the CastCut computer.
- Record of acceptance of the terms: 5 years after the contract ends (limitation period).
- Presentation material: as long as it serves to present CastCut Studio, or until the business asks (GTC 8.4).

## 5. Your rights

You can ask for information about your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of its processing, and object
to its processing. Address requests primarily to the business; the maker of CastCut Studio (horgaszvideo.bot@gmail.com)
passes them on and helps fulfil them. You can complain to the Hungarian data protection authority (NAIH, naih.hu)
or go to court.

## 6. Security

The bot never asks for a password. Access keys are kept in the computer's keychain, the control panel is reachable
only locally, and the bot only copies to Drive – it does not delete. Details: CastCut Studio's security notes.
