# Consent to appear in footage – template

**Version:** 1.0 · A free template for businesses (guests, customers, staff). Using this exact text is not
required; what matters is that consent can be shown. Translation; the Hungarian text ([HU](../hu/hozzajarulas-minta.md))
prevails.

## Short version (spoken / by message)
> "Do you agree that ⟨business⟩ may use your image and voice from today's footage on its social media and website,
> and that the video may also appear when CastCut Studio is presented? You can withdraw this at any time." –
> "Yes." (date, name)

## Written version
I, ⟨name⟩, agree that ⟨business name⟩ may use my image and voice in the photos and videos taken during ⟨date,
event⟩
- on the business's social media, website and advertisements, and
- ☐ when presenting CastCut Studio, which made the videos (social media, website, presentations)

without limits of time or territory and without payment. I understand that I can withdraw this consent at any time
at ⟨business contact⟩; withdrawal affects content already published as far as possible (the business removes it),
but not the lawfulness of use before the withdrawal.

Date and place: ⟨…⟩ · Signature: ⟨…⟩

## For minors
The consent above is given by the legal guardian (parent, guardian): "I, ⟨name⟩, as legal guardian of ⟨minor's
name⟩, agree …"

## Standing consent (regular staff)
Regular staff can give consent once for all footage taken at work; this can be recorded in the CastCut Studio
project, so the bot does not ask for it per video.
